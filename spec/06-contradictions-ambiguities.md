# 06 — Contradiction / Ambiguity Report

Findings from the consistency pass (Phase 4 of the master prompt). Severity: **BLOCKING** (implementation cannot proceed without a decision), **MAJOR** (risk of divergent implementations), **MINOR** (presentation/terminology), **INFO** (supersession record, no action).

Resolution statuses:
- **resolved-by-later-text**: the source's own later frozen text resolves it (recorded, no decision needed).
- **superseded**: earlier text is obsolete; canonical spec uses the later form (recorded only).
- **open**: requires an explicit architectural decision → also listed in `09-unresolved-decisions.md` with its `U-` ID.
- **resolved-by-addendum**: a post-audit frozen addendum in `spec/01` resolves it (recorded; the addendum is the decision).
