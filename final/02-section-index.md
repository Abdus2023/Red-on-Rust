## 5. Identifier namespaces in the FINAL1 set

Prefixes `R-` `S-` `C-` `U-` `M0NN` `T-` `N-` `X-` `V-` `D-` `HD-` `MOD-` `REQ-` `ROR-` `F-` (REF1) `F-INFL-` (V1) `GAP-` `DET-` `SEC-` `AMB-` `VU-` `CN-` are *inherited* and unrenumbered. Two namespaces are **additive at the compilation layer only**: `GI-SEC/DET/REC-nn` (global-invariant registry, `final/05`) and `FA-nn` (FINAL1 symbol-reuse records, §29/`final/09`). Additive IDs reference existing definitions; they never replace a `U-` or `C-` row, and they do not appear in `spec/`/`req/`/`mod/`/`dep/`/`term/`.
